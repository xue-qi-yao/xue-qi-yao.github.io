\section{Research Experience}

      {Graduate Research Assistant, with Prof.\ Wei Gao}{Aug 2024 -- Jul 2026}
\vspace{-0.4em}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Proposed PhyT2V, a training-free, LLM-guided self-refinement loop that improves the physical realism and temporal
        causality of text-to-video diffusion models (CVPR 2025, first author).
  \item Built InfiniBench, a procedurally generated benchmark for visual spatial reasoning with controllable scene
        complexity (CVPR 2026 Oral), and analyzed MLLM reasoning paths and latent states on multi-view spatial tasks (ECCV 2026, first author).
  \item Designed MMBERT, a Mixture-of-Experts multimodal BERT with a three-stage training recipe for robust hate speech
        detection under adversarial cloaking (AAAI 2026, first author).
\end{itemize}
